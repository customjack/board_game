    How To Build A Map

    1. Use the Map tab to add spaces, upload assets, and position everything on the board.
    2. Click a space to select it. Double-click a space to edit its visual settings and events.
    3. Use the Draw Connections viewport button, or hold Ctrl while dragging from one space to another, to create movement links.
    4. Click a connection to inspect it. Double-click a connection to edit its direction, color, visibility, or remove it.
    5. Fill out Metadata, Rules, and Engine tabs, then export the map when you are done.
